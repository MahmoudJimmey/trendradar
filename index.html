    Watch terms

    The Formats tab finds trends on its own, but detection only sees what the feeds already carry. When you spot something in the wild before it charts — in your For You feed, in a competitor's caption — add it to data/seeds.json. Every run probes those terms through TikTok's related-hashtag recommender and clusters anything matching onto a format row, so you catch it in week one instead of week three. Twenty live guesses beat two hundred stale ones.

    Source health

    Every source degrades on its own. A skipped source means a missing credential; a failed one means the endpoint changed or refused. Either way the numbers on screen are from the last successful run, not from now.
